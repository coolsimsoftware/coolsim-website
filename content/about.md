# About Applied Math Modeling, Inc.

Applied Math Modeling, Inc. (AMMCO) has been at the forefront of data center thermal modeling for over 17 years. Our flagship product, CoolSim, has helped engineers worldwide design more efficient data center cooling systems.

## Our Mission

**We believe engineers should own their simulation pipeline.**

Too many simulation tools lock you into proprietary formats and black-box solvers. CoolSim Next changes that by building on OpenFOAM, the industry-standard open-source CFD toolkit. You get enterprise-grade capabilities without vendor lock-in.

## The Evolution to CoolSim Next

For nearly two decades, CoolSim has been the trusted tool for data center thermal analysis. CoolSim Next represents a complete rethink of the platform, built from the ground up with modern web technologies and cloud-native architecture.

### What's New in CoolSim Next

- **Web-first architecture**: Run in your browser or as a desktop app
- **Real-time collaboration**: Multiple engineers can work on the same model
- **OpenFOAM foundation**: Full transparency and control over your solver pipeline
- **Modern UX**: Intuitive interface with intelligent automation
- **Flexible deployment**: SaaS, on-premises, or hybrid options

## Our Team

CoolSim Next is built by a team of engineers who understand the challenges of thermal modeling in real-world data centers. We combine deep domain expertise with modern software development practices to create tools that engineers actually want to use.

### Core Values

- **Transparency**: Open source solver, clear documentation
- **Ownership**: You control your models and simulation pipeline
- **Innovation**: Pushing the boundaries of what's possible in CFD
- **Community**: Supporting the broader data center engineering community
