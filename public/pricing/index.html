Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in all plans?

All plans include the full CoolSim Next modeling and simulation capabilities, including 3D modeling, CFD simulation, and thermal analysis. The difference is in the number of users, compute resources, and support level.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

Do you offer academic discounts?

Yes! We offer significant discounts for educational institutions and research projects. Contact our sales team for details.
